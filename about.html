About Horse & Rabbet Workshop

Horse & Rabbet Workshop is a custom shop specializing in handcrafted hardwood goods and interior woodwork from domestic walnut, maple, and oak. Whether you're looking for a unique piece for your home or a custom project, we'd love to bring your ideas to life.

FAQs

What does Horse & Rabbet mean?

The name comes from two things every woodshop relies on. The horse is the sawhorse, the sturdy, dependable foundation that holds the work steady. The rabbet is a type of joinery, a recess cut along the edge of a board so two pieces fit together precisely. Stable and precise: it's the same idea behind our tagline.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. We build custom projects, from interior woodwork to one-of-a-kind pieces for your home, in domestic walnut, maple, and oak. Tell us what you have in mind on our contact page and we'll be in touch.

Where are you located?

We're located in Leland, NC. Anything under 50 pounds can be shipped.

Are your cutting boards food safe?

Yes. Our cutting boards are made from solid domestic hardwoods and finished with a food-safe finish.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're picturing and we'll work with you on the design, the wood, and the finish.
